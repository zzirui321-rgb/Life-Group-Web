01 / RESEARCH

Shape electrons with light. Shape light with electrons.

We study controlled transfers of energy, momentum and information between electrons and light, probing the deeper structure of electromagnetic interactions. Combining theory and experiment, our work spans quantum measurement, topology, spacetime control and free-electron quantum optics.

01

Ultrafast, strong-field & free-electron quantum optics

Dielectric laser acceleration (DLA), free-electron radiation (FEL), photon-induced near-field electron microscopy (PINEM), attosecond dynamics and quantum weak measurement.

02

Topological photonics, photonic time crystals & quantum simulation

Floquet engineering, synthetic dimensions for free electrons, photonic spacetime crystals, momentum-gap solitons and nonlinear spacetime dynamics.

03

Condensed matter, quantum engineering & AI4Science

Weyl semimetals and quantum transport, quantum anomalies, free-electron condensed-matter physics, neural operators and Koopman operators.

04 / GROUP LEADER

Yiming Pan

Assistant Professor · Principal Investigator · PhD Supervisor

Yiming Pan has been based at the School of Physical Science and Technology and the Center for Transformative Science, ShanghaiTech University, Shanghai, China, since 2023. His interests include free-electron quantum optics, time-varying and topological photonics, and strong-field and quantum light–matter interactions.

06 / TEACHING & OUTREACH

Education for everyone.

To educate college students & kids: The first principle for me is to admit my stupidity in front of them.

To teach graduate students & postdocs: My job is to support them in achieving success in science and technology.

To teach the public: The most critical aspect of disseminating information to the public is determining: What is NOT science!

Equally access to education to all individuals without discrimination.

We teach and mentor undergraduate and graduate students, advise CUPT teams, serve as college mentors, and participate in campus open days. Through Bilibili videos, Zhihu columns, live science talks and science festivals, we share our curiosity about physics.
